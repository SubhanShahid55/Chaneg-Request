## Brand & Style

This design system channels the focused velocity of Linear paired with the structural clarity and calm restraint of Notion. Built for high-leverage product workflows, knowledge tools, and technical SaaS applications, the interface emphasizes quiet competence, spatial breathing room, and instantaneous legibility.

### Design Movement
The system balances **Minimalism** with **Modern Craft Utility**:
- **Structured Precision**: Uncluttered structural boundaries, strict alignment, and modular card-based containers.
- **Micro-tactility**: Subtle hairline borders, fine ambient elevation, and micro-interactions that feel crisp rather than decorative.
- **Quiet Canvas, Vivid Signals**: Monochromatic slate-zinc backgrounds allow content and status indicators to hold immediate semantic authority without visual noise.
